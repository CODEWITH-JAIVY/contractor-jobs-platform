[APP_NAME] connects workers with contractors. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Data we collect

  • Mobile number, name, city and role (worker or contractor) - for login and your profile.
  • Email address and Google account ID, only if you choose to log in with Google.
  • Contractor profile details (company name, work type, experience, about) and jobs posted.
  • Applications you make and follows you create.
  • Attendance, overtime and wage rates you enter in the Hisaab (diary) section. This is private to you and is not shown to contractors.
  • Payments/advances you record, and your worker profile (skills, experience, about). The worker profile is shown to a contractor only when you apply to that contractor's job.
  • Device notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) - to send job alerts.
  • Advertising identifiers collected by Google AdMob on the worker side of the app.

How we use it

To run the service: verify your number by OTP, show jobs, notify followers, and let contractors see applicants. When you apply to a job, the contractor who posted it can see your name, phone number and city.

Third parties

Firebase (notifications), an SMS provider (OTP delivery), Google AdMob (ads). They process data under their own policies.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all linked data inside the app (menu → Delete account), or see how to request deletion.
